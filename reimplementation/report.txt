================================================================================
Run date : 2026-05-07
Runtime  : ~3 min CPU
Cache    : results/fbcsp.pkl

Per-subject accuracy:
   Subject   FBCSP acc   Group
   -------   ---------   -----
   S01       0.6944      high
   S02       0.5347      low
   S03       0.7986      high
   S04       0.5868      low
   S05       0.5208      low
   S06       0.4062      low
   S07       0.7847      high
   S08       0.7118      high
   S09       0.6944      high
   -------   ---------
   mean      0.6370      (paper: 0.634)
   median    0.6944

Split (median = 0.6944):
   High = {1, 3, 7, 8, 9}   matches paper
   Low  = {2, 4, 5, 6}      matches paper

Aggregate confusion matrix (rows = true, cols = pred):
                Left   Right   Feet   Tongue
   Left          426    130     41      51
   Right          82    471     66      29
   Feet           91     62    367     128
   Tongue         89     92     80     387

Tongue <-> Feet is the dominant confusion (128 + 80) — expected for FBCSP
since both classes lack the lateralized C3/C4 ERD signature that mu/beta
CSP filters key on. Paper § 5.3 calls this out.

Pipeline reproduces paper baseline. Median split is locked in for the rest
of the experiments.

EXPERIMENT 2 — EEGNet (supervised, no augmentation)
================================================================================
Run date : 2026-05-07
Runtime  : 2.0 min  (A100 GPU, 300 epochs/subject)
Cache    : results/eegnet_noaug.pkl

Per-subject accuracy:
   Subject   EEGNet    FBCSP    Δ
   -------   ------    -----    -------
   S01       0.8160    0.6944   +0.1215
   S02       0.5556    0.5347   +0.0208
   S03       0.8750    0.7986   +0.0764
   S04       0.6215    0.5868   +0.0347
   S05       0.6493    0.5208   +0.1285
   S06       0.5625    0.4062   +0.1562
   S07       0.6979    0.7847   -0.0868
   S08       0.8056    0.7118   +0.0938
   S09       0.7778    0.6944   +0.0833
   -------   ------    -----    -------
   mean      0.7068    0.6370   +0.0698

Mean gain over FBCSP, by group (FBCSP-defined split, never recomputed):
   high performers {1, 3, 7, 8, 9}: +0.0576
   low  performers {2, 4, 5, 6}   : +0.0851
   low minus high                  : +0.0274
   verdict                         : LOW benefit MORE — supports BCI-inefficiency hypothesis

Aggregate confusion matrix (rows = true, cols = pred):
                Left   Right   Feet   Tongue
   Left          455     69     71     53
   Right          61    461     75     51
   Feet           41     54    470     83
   Tongue         54     83     65    446

Notes:
- Mean accuracy 0.7068 lands in the expected EEGNet range (0.71–0.74) — close
  to the lower end. Pipeline is faithful.
- Tongue/feet confusion drops sharply vs FBCSP (FBCSP: 128+80; EEGNet: 65+83 ≈
  similar pair magnitude but FBCSP's 128 leak collapses to 83). EEGNet handles
  the lateralized classes (left/right) much better — diagonal is more even.
- S07 is the only subject who LOST accuracy moving from FBCSP to EEGNet
  (-0.0868). That's a high performer regressing — worth tracking through
  ATCNet/Mamba to see if the regression persists or it's an EEGNet-specific
  issue.
